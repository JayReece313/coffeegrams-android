# CoffeeGrams for Android — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 14, 2026
**Developer:** JR Labs LLC

## The short version

**CoffeeGrams does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal
information.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
trackers. The only non-Android-platform library the app includes is Google
Play Billing, used solely to process the one-time Pro purchase (see below) —
it does not collect data on our behalf.

## What data the app handles

- **Your brew log** (the brews you save, along with any ratings and notes) is
  stored **only on your device**, in a local Room (SQLite) database. It never
  leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Purchases.** The one-time "CoffeeGrams Pro" purchase is processed entirely by
  **Google Play Billing**. We never see or receive your payment details.
- **Notifications.** Cold-brew and French-press reminders are **local
  notifications** scheduled on your device, not sent through any server. While a
  guided brew is actively running, the app also keeps a low-priority ongoing
  notification visible (an Android foreground service) so the timer can keep
  counting if you switch away — this is purely to keep the brew session alive
  on-device and involves no data leaving your device either.

## What we do NOT do

- We do **not** collect personal data.
- We do **not** use analytics or advertising.
- We do **not** track you across apps or websites.
- We do **not** use any third-party SDKs beyond Google Play Billing, which
  only processes the one-time Pro purchase.

## Children

CoffeeGrams is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
